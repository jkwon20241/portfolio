MÆZE

UX/UIwayfinding

A wayfinding prototype for the Providence Athenæum, made with the team ÆEEEE. Six questions lead the visitor from the card catalog, through the stacks and into one book, narrowing some 176,800 volumes to a single word. App prototype, iconography, poster, QR card and didactic. Type in Goudy Bookletter 1911 and Forevs.
