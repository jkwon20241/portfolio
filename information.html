Jennie Kwon is a Korean-American designer.

She is in her third year at the Rhode Island School of Design studying Graphic Design with a minor in Computation, Technology, and Culture. Jennie currently works as a Graphic Designer at Better World by Design and a Designer at The College Hill Independent.

Her work explores materiality and directionality in typography, examining how type and texture can bridge digital and physical making. Moving across motion, data manipulation, and vernacular signage, she approaches type as both kinetic and tactile.
